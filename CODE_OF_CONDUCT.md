# Code of Conduct

Open Source BYTE 2K26 is where a lot of people make their first-ever contribution. Everyone here is learning, including the maintainers.
This only works if it feels safe to ask "basic" questions and to get things wrong.

## We expect you to

- **Be kind and patient**, especially with beginners. Explain, don't mock.
- **Assume good intent.** A blunt review comment is usually just a busy person.
- **Give helpful feedback.** Say what to change and why, not just "this is wrong".
- **Credit others.** Don't copy someone else's work or take over an issue someone else has claimed.
- **Respect people's time.** Keep PRs focused and reply when someone asks you something.

## Not okay, anywhere in this project

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private messages
- Comments about someone's gender, caste, religion, region, language, appearance, or ability
- Sharing someone's personal information without permission
- Spam, including low-effort PRs made only to win points
- Pressuring people for answers or reviews

This applies to issues, pull requests, Discussions, code comments, and club channels connected to the program.

## If something happens

Contact a maintainer listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), email the club at [thebyteclub@nie.ac.in](mailto:thebyteclub@nie.ac.in), or speak to any BYTE Club core team member in person.
Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close PRs, or block people from the project, depending on what happened.

Based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
